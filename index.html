Questions people actually ask

Does it hurt?

The needles are about the width of a hair, and nothing like the ones used for shots or blood draws. You will not feel most of them go in. When you do, it is a quick pinch at first, then a dull heaviness or warmth around the point. That sensation is the treatment working. Most people fall asleep during treatment.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on how long you've had the problem. Recent injuries often turn around in a few visits. Something you've carried for years usually needs weekly visits for a month or two before spacing out. You should feel some change within the first two or three treatments, and if you don't, she'll tell you rather than keep booking you.
